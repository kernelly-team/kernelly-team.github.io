+ (04)ServicesSix Systems +

Six Services

Six systems, one studio.
01

AI Customer Chat Assistants

WhatsApp + Website

Customers wait hours for answers you repeat every day. An assistant trained on your own FAQs, price lists, and policies replies in seconds, and hands off to a human when it cannot help. You approve every answer source before it goes live.

Example PilotA booking assistant for a clinic: hours, prices, appointments, all day. (Illustration, not a past client.)
02

Workflow Automation

n8n

Your team loses hours to copy-paste work between apps. We rebuild the routine as an n8n automation that runs on its own: data moved, sheets updated, invoices generated. A simple runbook, so you are never locked in.

Example PilotNew orders land in a tracking sheet, customers get confirmations, you get a daily summary. (Illustration, not a past client.)
03

Content Automation

On-brand posts + product content

Consistent content dies to the daily grind. A pipeline drafts on-brand posts from your own notes and past content, on a weekly rhythm. You review every draft before anything ships.

Example PilotA week of post drafts every Sunday, in one document, ready for your review. (Illustration, not a past client.)
04

Lead Research & Follow-up

Real prospects + tracked follow-ups

Good prospects slip by unresearched and unfollowed. A list of real, publicly reachable prospects, each with a source and a fit note, and every follow-up tracked. Draft first messages you can edit and own.

Example PilotFifty businesses that publicly asked for your kind of help, each with a contact route. (Illustration, not a past client.)
05

Reporting Dashboards

Your numbers in one live view

Your numbers live in five places, and none are current. One live dashboard pulls from your sheets, store, and ad accounts, around the few numbers you actually decide on. A plain-language summary lands in your inbox on a schedule.

Example PilotThis week's sales, top products, and ad spend vs last week, refreshed every Monday. (Illustration, not a past client.)
06

First-Run Onboarding

New users reach their first win

New users drift away before their first win. A screen-by-screen teardown of the first run, then fixes: checklists, emails, in-product nudges. Judged on your own activation numbers.

Example PilotA SaaS first-15-minutes teardown: three drop-off points and a concrete fix for each. (Illustration, not a past client.)
