Demo samples

Two sample builds, shown as samples.

Pages we designed and built ourselves to show how we work: layout, typography, mobile behaviour, forms and the small details. They are demo projects, not client work.

Plainly: neither page is a client project. There is no client, no rating, no review and no result claimed anywhere on them. Where a real site would carry a testimonial, a review count or a patient number, these pages show visible placeholders instead. They are marked “Demo sample” on the page itself.
